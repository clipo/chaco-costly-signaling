# Reference Index

Master lookup table for all sources used in the Chaco Canyon Costly Signaling project. Search by tags or keywords to find the relevant paper, then check for a summary or claims extraction.

## Format

```
filename | authors_year | tags | key_content
```

## Tag Vocabulary

- `critique`: Papers critiquing or responding to other claims
- `methodology`: Papers primarily about methods
- `review`: Review articles or meta-analyses
- `empirical`: Papers presenting primary data
- `modeling`: Computational or statistical modeling papers
- `ethnohistoric`: Ethnohistoric or ethnographic accounts
- `chronology`: Dating evidence
- `paleoclimate`: Climate reconstruction and drought data
- `construction`: Great house construction chronology and architecture
- `exchange`: Long-distance trade and exotic goods
- `population`: Population estimates and settlement patterns
- `signaling`: Costly signaling theory and applications
- `conflict`: Violence, warfare, and social control

- `pilgrimage`: Pilgrimage models and movement
- `cooperation`: Cooperation, exchange networks, risk buffering

1212468.pdf | Enquist_Leimar_1983 | signaling, modeling, conflict | FOUNDATIONAL PAPER for Layer 2. Introduces the sequential assessment model of animal contests. Fights consist of repeated costly interactions; at each step, opponents acquire noisy observations of relative fighting ability theta = ln(c_B/c_A) with noise sigma. ESS is a switching line in causal factor space (sampling average x, step count n); pure and unique for given c and sigma. Key results: assessment produces "good fighting economy" (expected cost 0.049 vs. resource value 1.0, expected utility 0.451); contests between equally matched opponents are costliest (peak cost at theta=0); higher sigma produces longer/costlier fights (Table 1: cost rises from 0.029 at sigma=0.5 to 0.063 at sigma=1.5 for c=0.005); war of attrition (no assessment) yields zero expected utility; assessment is evolutionarily stable against convention-based alternatives. Causal factor state compresses to sufficient statistic (x, n). Monument signals map as sigma-reducing technology: pre-contest information about group quality substitutes for costly assessment rounds.
