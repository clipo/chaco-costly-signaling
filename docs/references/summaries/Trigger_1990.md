## Relevance to Argument

Trigger 1990 provides the foundational materialist argument that monumental architecture is best understood as conspicuous consumption of energy, with energy control serving as the fundamental measure of power. This framing anticipates the costly signaling interpretation by identifying energy expenditure (cost) as the currency of architectural power symbolism. The paper is a key precursor to formal CST applications to monuments (Glatz & Plourde 2011, Kantner & Vaughn 2012) and provides cross-cultural justification for treating construction cost as a signal rather than an incidental feature of monument building.

## Methodological Notes

- The "thermodynamic" framing is metaphorical, using energy expenditure as a cross-culturally commensurable measure of monument cost.
- The paper predates formal game-theoretic applications of signaling to archaeology.
- The energy framework provides a common metric (Joules, person-hours) that the mls-monuments model operationalizes as C.
- 281 CrossRef citations indicate high influence in the field.
