## Gap Notes

- **Chaco-specific construction energetics:** No source provides person-day labor estimates for Chaco great houses. Search "Chaco great house labor estimate" or check Lekson's early publications.
- **Bet-hedging theory (formal):** No sources from evolutionary ecology on bet-hedging as distinct from risk-spreading. Search "Seger Brockmann 1987 bet hedging," "Starrfelt Kokko 2012."
- **Post-collapse violence osteology:** Need Kuckelman et al. on Castle Rock Pueblo and Turner & Turner on Southwest cannibalism evidence.
- **Multilevel selection / Price equation in archaeology:** No sources applying the Price equation to archaeological cases. Search "Price equation archaeology," "Okasha multilevel selection."
- **Partner choice / network formation models:** Formal evolutionary game theory models of network formation through signaling. Search "partner choice cooperation," "McNamara partner choice."
- **PDSI-construction chronology correlation:** No study directly correlating annual PDSI with construction episodes. This may be original analysis for this project.
